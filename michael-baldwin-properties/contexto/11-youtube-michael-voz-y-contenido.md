## Voz de Michael: lo observado (citas de las transcripciones)
- **Educa, no vende.** *"I look at my role as just like an educator. I I educate and inform them. I give them enough information to make a sound decision."* [Michael Baldwin in Los Cabos 1/2, 5:13]. *"They don't they don't need to be sold."* Coincide con el Brief: *"We never use aggressive sales tactics or urgency."* [05 Q5].
- **Familia y experiencia.** *"The reason people buy the real estate here is they want to be with their families"*, *"drawing on the chalkboard of memories"* [Top Real Estate Broker]. En tours: *"Imagine, if you will, you and your family and all of your friends arriving to this one spot."* [Casa Amore, 0:45].
- **Su propia historia como argumento.** *"We knew no one when we moved here."*, *"when we moved here from Houston we don't even lock our doors here in Cabo"*, *"I'm from Houston."*
- **Las personas locales como diferenciador.** *"No one has our secret weapon, which is the local people."* [1/2, 2:55].
- **Conveniencia y geografía.** *"two-hour flight time of Houston, Dallas, LA, San Diego."* [1/2, 1:34]. Es coherente con los mercados del Brief, pero no respalda un porcentaje.
- **Habla concreto y práctico.** *"What that means to you is that when you need parking, overnight parking, you receive your guests…"* [Casa Amore, 0:20]. Invita en primera persona: *"I want to personally invite you to come and see this house."*
- **Cercanía con el cliente.** *"The owners of this home are personal friends of ours."*, *"these people that I sell are my neighbors. So, they become my friends."*
- **Confiado y humilde a la vez.** *"I'm the top producer in all of Southern B[aja]."*, *"we're the number one broker in town and by dollar volume and I'm the number one agent."*, pero también *"I'm very humbled by the fact that I get a chance to work with these type of clients."*
- **Frase propia que puede chocar con el cliente:** *"my clients are 1% of the 1%."* El Brief pide *"Aspirational without being boastful"* [06 Q4].
- **Los subtítulos tienen errores** ("Delmare", "wine celler", "Southern B."); no copiar frases de ahí sin revisarlas.

## Diferencias y coincidencias con el Brief y con lo aprobado (las diferencias, por resolver con el cliente)
1. **Total vendido:** lista de YouTube *"more than 1.75 B in Sales"*; Facebook *"$1.8 billion"*; Instagram *"$2B+"*; Brief *"Over US$2 billion"* [01 Q4].
2. **Precio de Casa Amore:** la descripción dice *"US$13,900,000"* y el video dice *"This house is 15.9 million"*. Hay que confirmar cuál es.
3. **Correo en las descripciones:** *"michael@delmarre.com"*. El correo oficial aprobado es michael@michaelbaldwinproperties.com.
4. **"#1" y su alcance:** los videos dicen *"top producer in all of Southern B"* y *"top-selling real estate agent in all of Baja California Sur"*; el Brief lo respalda como *"No. 1 in sales volume in Baja California Sur, according to MLS BCS data"* [01 Q4].
5. **Fundación de Del Mar:** el video dice *"I was one of the founding members in 2014"*, que coincide con el Brief (*"In 2014 Michael became a founding member of Del Mar Real Estate"* [01 Q1]).
6. **Llegada en 2010:** el video dice *"I moved to Cabo in 2010"*; Daniela lo confirmó. El Brief detalla que primero rentaron en Cabo San Lucas y luego pasaron a Palmilla [01 Q1].
7. **Tono promocional del canal:** *"Discover the energy. Feel the luxury. Live the lifestyle."*, *"this is your sign"* [descripción del video "The Life You've Been Dreaming Of"]. El Brief pide evitar *"Generic superlatives and overused expressions"* y un tono demasiado casual [06 Q5].
8. **Contacto en las descripciones:** apuntan a lifestyleincabo.com y al Instagram michaelbaldwinproperties; todavía no a MichaelBaldwinProperties.com.
