## 01 — The Business & Its Story


### 1. How did Michael Baldwin Properties originate, and what led to its creation?

Michael's path to Los Cabos was deliberate rather than accidental. In Houston he was both a home builder and land developer and one of the market's top-grossing luxury brokers. This combination still shapes how he advises clients on value, construction quality and replacement cost. When the 2008 financial crisis hit, he closed his Houston business and, together with his now-wife Stephanie (also a Houstonian), began looking for a beachfront "pre-retirement" base outside the United States.

The search took three years. They visited virtually every beach destination within a four-hour flight of Houston, across the Caribbean, Central and South America and Mexico, and compared them on currency, language, medical infrastructure and ease of access. Los Cabos won on fundamentals.

In 2010 they rented a home in Cabo San Lucas without knowing anyone locally. A friend introduced Michael to Ron Hatfield, a fellow Houstonian and the developer behind Villas del Mar in Palmilla. The two connected immediately, and Hatfield invited Michael to join him. That introduction is how the story moved from San Lucas to Palmilla, and it marks the beginning of Michael Baldwin Properties: the practice has been operating for 16 years, since 2010.

In 2014 Michael became a founding member of Del Mar Real Estate with Mr. Hatfield, which has been MBP's broker of record ever since. Lifestyle in Cabo® by Michael Baldwin has been the flagship brand of Michael's own advisory and marketing platform.


### 2. How would you describe the business today? What are its principal services and areas of specialization?

MBP is a boutique ultra-luxury real estate advisory practice in Palmilla, Los Cabos, built around Michael's personal expertise and supported by a small, cross-functional team. Its principal services are:

- Buyer advisory and representation for resale homes, new construction and home sites in Palmilla.
- Seller representation and listing marketing, including valuation (sales comparison plus replacement-cost analysis), positioning, incremental marketing on top of Del Mar's standard package, and monthly listing reports to every seller.
- Market intelligence: proprietary transaction data, an annual Market Study and pricing guidance.
- Post-purchase continuity through the Del Mar ecosystem, including property management, bill payment, and oversight while owners are away (Del Mar Management), as well as facilitating access to exclusive membership opportunities such as Club Ninety Six and Palmilla Golf Club and Palmilla Social Club.
- Lifestyle access and curation through Lifestyle in Cabo (yachting, dining, clubs and experiences).
Specialization: Palmilla's ultra-luxury segment, meaning Villas del Mar, and other Palmilla Communities. The 2026–27 portfolio so far comprises 19 listings across Villas del Mar, Querencia, Palmilla Cove, Oasis and Espíritu del Mar.

Team: Michael Baldwin (Founder & Principal Advisor); Stephanie Baldwin (Business Partner & Public Relations); Olivia Bailly (Real Estate Advisor, full-time in sales since 2024, co-lists select properties and fronts Lifestyle In Cabo activities and rentals advisory); Lina González (Sales Operations Office Manager), Marian Padilla (Marketing & Operations Coordinator).

### 2. What role would you like Lifestyle in Cabo to play moving forward: an editorial platform, a lifestyle resource, a lead-generation channel, or something else?

Lifestyle in Cabo should serve as both a lifestyle resource and a lead-generation channel, introducing affluent audiences to Los Cabos and nurturing relationships long before they are ready to make a real estate decision.

Its focus is the lifestyle of affluent residents, visitors, and prospective buyers in Los Cabos: dining and activity recommendations, experiences, events, communities, and the rhythm of life here. Through a more relaxed, approachable, and lifestyle-oriented voice, the brand builds familiarity with the destination and trust over time. It should also actively promote real estate listings and generate leads, with a primary focus on properties in the US$5 million to US$10 million range, while remaining relevant to a broader affluent audience.

MichaelBaldwinProperties.com, currently being developed on BoomTown, serves a complementary purpose: it is the dedicated real estate advisory and sales platform for clients actively exploring acquisition opportunities. Listings, pricing, market intelligence, the Market Study, and Michael’s market commentary will be central to this platform, which is designed to attract qualified leads and connect prospective buyers with Michael and the team.

The two brands should work together without being interchangeable or hierarchical. Lifestyle in Cabo engages audiences earlier in their journey, while Michael Baldwin Properties provides a more focused, expert-led experience for clients evaluating significant real estate investments, particularly in the US$10 million to US$30 million range. Both brands should contribute to listing exposure and lead generation, each through its own positioning, audience, and communication style.


### 3. How should Michael's personal expertise and reputation support the Michael Baldwin Properties brand?

Michael is the brand's principal proof point. Buyers trust him because he made the same decision they are considering: he studied every alternative, chose Cabo, lives in Palmilla and has his own capital invested there. His expertise should anchor the brand through:

- Signature market commentary (the "Advisor Notes" concept): unscripted, data-backed perspectives on pricing, scarcity and buyer psychology.
- Press and interviews positioning him as the authority on Los Cabos ultra-luxury real estate.
- Personal presence at key client moments, with the team handling day-to-day execution.
At the same time, the brand should make it clear that clients are served by a team and a methodology, not by one person alone.


### 4. How would you describe the relationship between Michael Baldwin Properties and Del Mar Real Estate? Are there any branding, communication, or operational guidelines we should consider?

Branding, Advertising & MLS Guidelines

Del Mar RE is currently developing formal branding and advertising guidelines, which are expected to be shared in the coming weeks. These guidelines will also align with applicable MLS rules.

Based on the preliminary guidance shared by Marketing, the key considerations include:

- Brokerage identification: When agents use personal branding, dedicated websites, or custom URLs, the approved brokerage name and logo must be prominently displayed. Personal branding must remain subordinate in size and prominence to the brokerage’s branding.
- Advertising requirements: Communications and advertising must use the sponsoring brokerage’s official registered company name, as required by the applicable guidelines.
- MLS compliance: Agent or broker contact information may not be included in the MLS listing description field.
These requirements are still being formalized. The Brandgger should take them into account when developing MBP’s brand identity, website, marketing materials, and communication strategy, while allowing for adjustments once the final guidelines are issued.

Our objective is to establish a distinctive and independent identity for Michael Baldwin Properties while ensuring that its branding, advertising, and marketing activities remain consistent with Del Mar RE’s requirements and applicable MLS rules to be confirmed.

## 04 — Target Audience & Buyer Insights


### 1. Who are your most valuable clients today? Please describe their typical background, interests, and buying motivations.

Ultra-high-net-worth individuals and families, typically founders, executives and investors aged roughly 45–68 (our internal archetype is "The Strategic Lifestyle Investor"). They usually buy with cash or minimal financing and have a 1-month to 2-year purchase window.

- Interests: family, health and fitness, longevity, golf, tennis and pickleball, boating, and dining.
- Travel: many fly private or semi-private (e.g., JSX), but their children and grandchildren rely on direct commercial flights. Flight connectivity to their home city therefore matters.
- Motivations: a family gathering place, a community of like-minded peers, and a well-protected long-term asset.
- Behavior: they are spending far more time in Cabo than before, some now six to seven months a year, compared with the short weekend stays of a decade ago.
- They value privacy, low risk and minimal friction ("they don't want risk and they don't want brain damage").

### 2. Which geographic markets currently generate the strongest opportunities? Which would you like to develop further?

- Strongest: Texas, particularly Houston and Dallas, which represents roughly 55% of our CRM database. California (especially the Bay Area) is the second key market.
- Also active: Colorado and New York; Canada and Europe to a lesser extent.
- To develop: the Silicon Valley tech-founder cluster already present in the community; other UHNW U.S. markets with direct flights to SJD. We maintain a ranked analysis of the top 30 U.S. feeder markets by UHNWI concentration and flight connectivity, available on request.

### 3. What are the most common reasons clients purchase in Los Cabos: lifestyle, second-home ownership, relocation, family use, investment, retirement, or other motivations?

Primarily second- and third-home ownership for lifestyle and family use, increasingly with extended seasonal stays. Investment logic is important but secondary: buyers want confidence that value is protected (scarcity, rising replacement cost), not short-term yield. Pre-retirement and partial relocation are growing, as reflected in Michael's own story.

Why Los Cabos (Michael's view): proximity and flight access to the U.S., language, currency, medical infrastructure, safety and a maturing destination (airport, roads, dining and events). Michael believes Cabo will become the No. 1 destination in Mexico for Americans.


### 4. What property types, communities, and approximate price ranges are most relevant to your target audience?

- Palmilla: entry point about US$4M; average almost US$9M; high-water mark about US$45M
- Focus band for 2026–27: US$10M to US$30M+.
- Our internal tiers: top tier US$15M+, medium US$8M–15M, entry tier below US$8M.
- New adopters typically enter at US$5M–8M, often buying the resale homes vacated by upgrading owners.
- Product trend: larger homes that function as primary residences, with big closets, bathrooms, outdoor spaces and chef's kitchens, and higher bedroom counts to host extended family and staff.

### 5. What concerns, questions, or objections do buyers typically express before purchasing?

- The perceived risk of buying a US$5M–7M+ home in another country ("a really daunting task," in Michael's words).
- Safety, and whether they will tire of the destination.
- Infrastructure: water and electricity capacity in a fast-growing desert destination. Villas del Mar is addressing this with its own desalination plant.
- Who will look after the home when they are away.
- The appeal of branded residences (Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis) as a perceived risk-mitigation shortcut.
- The cost and timeline of new construction, given labor and material inflation, versus buying resale.

### 6. What makes a buyer feel confident enough to move forward with a transaction?

- First, deciding that Palmilla is where they want to live and belong. Michael insists this precedes the investment conversation.
- Seeing that existing owners keep reinvesting and upgrading.
- Clear value logic: current resales at about US$1,200–1,800 per sq ft versus roughly US$2,200–2,300 per sq ft for new construction, in locations that cannot be replicated.
- A full-service platform that removes the burden of ownership.
- The community itself: meeting future neighbors at the beach club, gym and golf club.
- Michael's personal credibility as a fellow buyer and resident.

### 7. How do your buyer profiles differ from your seller profiles, and which audience should be prioritized in the next stage of growth?

Sellers in our segment are typically long-standing Palmilla owners who are upgrading within the community, consolidating or exiting. Many bought 10–30 years ago and now own older homes in prime locations. Buyers are either existing owners moving up or new arrivals entering at the US$5M–8M level.

Sellers judge us on evidence of marketing investment and results, which is why every seller receives a monthly listing report. Buyers judge us on access, insight and trust.

Priority for the next stage: buyers first. Buyer demand is what wins listings, so the brand should lead with buyer-facing authority, while maintaining a dedicated track of proof for sellers (results, marketing investment and monthly reporting).


### 8. Could you describe two or three representative client experiences, without sharing confidential information?

Illustrative profiles (drawn from patterns Michael describes, not specific clients)

- The upgrader: a Texas family that bought a US$4M–5M home in Palmilla years ago, grew comfortable with the destination and community, and is now moving up to an US$8M–10M estate built for extended stays and visiting family.
- The new adopter: a first-time Cabo buyer who enters at US$5M–8M, buying the resale an upgrading owner has vacated, often a well-located older home they will remodel.
- The value buyer: a client with about US$5M who acquires a second-row, full-ocean-view resale for less than its replacement cost and modernizes it, much like buying an older home in Beverly Hills for the location.

## 05 — Client Experience & Service Philosophy


### 1. How would you describe the experience of working with Michael Baldwin Properties, from the first inquiry through closing and beyond?

Personal, discreet and unhurried. It begins with understanding how the family wants to live and continues with private tours of homes and the community. Then comes data-backed guidance on value and negotiation, followed by a closing coordinated through Del Mar Real Estate's transaction infrastructure. After closing, clients can rely on property management and introductions to the community.


### 2. What level of involvement and personal attention can clients expect from Michael and the team?

Michael works directly with buyers himself; he is not a figurehead. Olivia Bailly co-represents select clients and listings, and the operations team supports transaction coordination, documentation and communications.


### 3. What services or guidance do you provide beyond property selection and negotiation?

- Valuation guidance, including replacement-cost analysis and renovation or rebuild potential.
- Market intelligence: an annual Market Study and ongoing insight.
- Referrals to trusted attorneys and notaries, including the bank trust (fideicomiso) structure for foreign buyers.
- Referrals to architects, builders and interior designers for remodels and new builds.
- Property management, oversight and bill payment through Del Mar Management.
- Rental management for owners who want income while they are away.
- Introductions to clubs and the community (Palmilla Golf Club, Club Ninety Six, Club Espíritu and the Palmilla Social Club).
- Lifestyle concierge for our clients: recommendations for dining, activities, private and ground transportation, and yachting, delivered through Lifestyle in Cabo.
- Yachting: Michael founded the Villas del Mar Yacht Community and hosts its annual event, which celebrates its 5th anniversary this season. Owners who have a boat, or would like one, benefit from his first-hand insight.
- Monthly listing reports for sellers.

### 4. How do you support international buyers who may be unfamiliar with the Mexican real estate market?

Michael's own experience as an American buyer is the foundation: he understands the hesitation first-hand. We guide clients through the destination's fundamentals and the community, and through Del Mar's established transaction and property management infrastructure.

In practice, we connect clients with trusted attorneys and notaries, explain the bank trust (fideicomiso) through which foreign buyers hold property near the coast, and coordinate the closing. After closing, property management, rental management and our lifestyle concierge make ownership from abroad effortless.


### 5. What role do discretion, privacy, trust, and long-term relationships play in your service approach?

They are central. Our clients are often public figures or business leaders who expect confidentiality, and many of our relationships span multiple transactions and generations. Michael notes that some Palmilla families are now into their fourth generation. Michael's own close friendships, including travel with neighbors, show how relationships extend beyond the transaction. We never use aggressive sales tactics or urgency.


### 6. What do you want clients to remember or say about their experience after completing a transaction?

"Michael and his team made it effortless, told us the truth, and brought us into a community that changed how our family spends its time." Or, in Michael's words: "This real estate will change your life."

### 4. What qualities should always be reflected in the brand's communication?

- Strategic, data-backed and authoritative.
- White-glove, discreet and client-first.
- An insider's perspective.
- Aspirational without being boastful.
- Specific and detail-rich.

### 5. Are there words, expressions, visual styles, or common luxury real estate clichés you would prefer to avoid?

- Sales-driven language: Mass-market messaging, aggressive calls to action, urgency tactics and pressure selling. The brand should communicate through expertise and trust, not sales pressure.
- Empty luxury clichés: Generic superlatives and overused expressions that lack substance or could apply to any luxury property. We prefer specific insights, meaningful context and credible information.
- Predictable marketing language: Avoid overused phrases such as “front-row seat,” “the journey behind the numbers” and “the evolution of the market.” The voice should feel distinctive, intelligent and authentic to Michael and his perspective.
- Tone: Avoid language that feels overly casual or lacks the sophistication expected in high-value real estate advisory.
- Visual direction: Avoid cluttered layouts, heavy graphic overlays and generic luxury aesthetics. The visual identity should feel sophisticated, distinctive and contemporary, rather than relying on predictable luxury design conventions.
- Professional representation: Michael and Olivia should always be presented as a team, with imagery and messaging that reflect their respective roles and professional working relationship.
- Credibility: No unverified claims, unsupported market statistics or artificial scarcity. All statements about market conditions, demand, availability and performance must be substantiated.

Authority leads; lifestyle provides the evidence.
